# Acknowledgements & Credits

The development of the **Laravel Agent-Debugger** was made possible by drawing inspiration from modern profiling components and communities in the Laravel eco-system:

---

## Technical Inspiration

*   **Laravel Debugbar (Barry vd. Heuvel)**: The foundational inspiration for capturing queries, view files, and system exceptions during requests.
*   **Laravel Telescope**: Inspired our structured logger backend approach, proving that server-driven records are far more secure and portable than heavy inline scripts.
*   **VitePress (VueJS Community)**: For providing an exceptionally lightweight, fast, and gorgeous markdown-based documentation engine.

---

## Active Contributors

Special thanks to:
*   **The Google DeepMind Team**: For developing the agentic AI architectures that guided our robust diagnostic profiling specifications.
*   **The Laravel Community**: For continuously advocating for zero-JS, performant, and environment-aware developer tooling.
